# Lumi — Creative & Narrative Collection

## Story — The House of Returning Light
At dusk, a housekeeper lit a lamp in every room where someone had left a question. Some rooms held answers; others held only a chair and a window. Each morning she opened the shutters and wrote down which light had returned, which had faded, and which had never been lit. The house became a place where unfinished things could remain visible without being mistaken for failures.
