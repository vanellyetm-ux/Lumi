## What each seed contains
Each proposal adds a short original story, a visual-art direction, an original music sketch, and an illustrative structured-data representation (§).

## Stewardship
These are proposals on review branches, not merged publications. Confirm authorship and license before reusing outside this collection. Sensitive or private material belongs in appropriately restricted repositories, never in this public index.
