## Stream position

`ORIGIN / SOURCE REPOSITORIES → DATA ENVELOPE MINT / MUSIC CREATION → $ILK MIRROR / PROVENANCE → LUMI / MAIN STREAM → HUMAN-APPROVED PRESENTATION`

## What flows through the stream

- Original lyrics and rhyme schemes
- Beat maps, meter, rhythm, and tempo structures
- ABC, LilyPond, and MusicXML notation
- MIDI-ready arrangement data
- Rendered WAV masters and stems
- .PhD research notes, methods, and source citations
- Validation reports, checksums, and release receipts

## Visibility boundary

DataEnvelopeMint is private. This public frame is an index, not a mirror of private files. It exposes only approved descriptions and links; private lyrics, research files, stems, and source materials stay in their authorized repositories unless separately approved for publication.

## Promotion ladder

`DRAFT → SOURCE_VERIFIED → COMPOSED → ARRANGED → RENDERED → EVIDENCE_REVIEWED → HUMAN_APPROVED → PUBLISHED`

Every public release must have a verified output, documented rights status, provenance record, and human approval. No automatic publication is implied by this index.
